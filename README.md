# RIISE GenUI — interactive prototype

A clickable proof of concept of the RIISE app with a chat-first **AI view** next to the classic screens.

- Open the site from this repository's GitHub Pages link. Use the **Mobile / Web** switch to change layouts and the **Classic / AI** toggle in the header to switch views.
- All data is sample data captured from a UAT test account. Personal details are removed.
- Nothing is real: orders, SIPs, alerts and subscriptions are **simulated** and never sent anywhere.

Try in the AI view: “Show my portfolio”, “Create a watchlist with Infosys, TCS, Parag Parikh Flexi Cap and Motilal Oswal Midcap”, “Buy 10 Infosys”, “Compare my funds with mid cap and small cap funds”, “Alert me if any of my bank stocks falls 5% in a day”, “Mail me research reports on Infosys and TCS”, or ask in Hindi.
